# About

I build systems that turn pixels into intelligence — and intelligence back into
pixels. My day-to-day is somewhere between research code, GPU clusters, and
production data pipelines.

## what I work on

- Generative models — fine-tuning & deploying diffusion models (FLUX, SD, SDXL)
  with LoRA, evaluating them with VLM judges, and shipping the whole loop on
  SLURM + Airflow.
- Computer vision & robotics — past life in dense visual odometry, SLAM,
  and 3D reconstruction. I still write a lot of CUDA when no one is looking.
- MLOps at scale — content-addressed dataset stores, deduplication,
  reproducible runs, and pipelines that don't fall over at 3 a.m.

## philosophy

> If you can't reproduce it, you didn't do it.

I like small, sharp tools, plain text, and READMEs that respect your time.
